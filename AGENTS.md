# AGENTS.md — music & YouTube channel videos

Notes for AI agents working with Thor's music (the songs in this repo) and his
YouTube channel (@RealThorwegian).

## The approved channel-video look (v5, Thor, 18 Sep 2026)

ffmpeg `showspectrum`, waterfall style, dual mirror:

- **orientation=horizontal** — the spectrum scrolls UP, not sideways
- **Colormap rotation (Thor, 19 Sep 2026):** fire, green, mako — random pick per
  video, fixed once rendered. `fire`/`green` are presets (`color=fire`,
  `color=green`); **mako** = `color=intensity` +
  `lut3d=file=/tmp/mako.cube:interp=trilinear` (33³ cube generated from
  matplotlib's mako on the Oracle box). Replaces the earlier cividis choice.
- `scale=log`, `mode=combined`, `legend=0`
- **Frequency range 0-5512 Hz** (`stop=5512`), quarter of the full band
- **RGB level curve, darkening direction**, applied to the spectrum only:
  `eq=gamma_r=0.4545:gamma_g=0.4545:gamma_b=0.4545` (per-channel gamma 0.4545
  = power 2.2). Thor's spec: "apply exp curve … RGB level curve … the other
  way". The brightening direction was rejected, so was master gamma.
- **Render 135x135**, `fps=60`, lanczos upscale to 1080x1080. The small
  render makes the vertical scroll ~4x faster than a 270 render.
- **Time mirror**: `vflip`, crop the top half, then vstack with its own
  `vflip`. New content is born at the horizontal centre seam; hits and claps
  appear at the picture centre with zero delay.
- **Frequency mirror**: hstack with `hflip`. The bass line rides the vertical
  centre axis. Combined result is a symmetric diamond.
- 60 fps, h264 ~8 Mbps (h264_videotoolbox on the Mac), **AAC 320k, 48 kHz,
  audio delayed 83 ms** (`-af "adelay=83|83"`, picture leads sound by 2/24 s —
  Thor's animation sync spec, 19 Sep 2026), `-movflags +faststart`.

### "Vertical centre = audio" (Thor's spec)

A white flash at the centre rows, driven by the bass + clap envelope (block
FFT, max-pooled to 60 fps, fast attack / ~120 ms release, percentile-scaled).
Generated by `gen_flash_centre.py` as RGBA raw frames sized **270x136**
(mirrored dimensions) and fed as a second input. The level curve applies to
the spectrum only; the flash is not affected.

### Reference chain (v5, full song)

```bash
ffmpeg -y -v error -i TRACK.mp3 \
  -f rawvideo -pix_fmt rgba -s 270x136 -r 60 -i /tmp/flash.raw \
  -filter_complex "[0:a]showspectrum=size=135x135:orientation=horizontal:mode=combined:color=cividis:scale=log:slide=rscroll:legend=0:stop=5512,fps=60,format=rgb24,eq=gamma_r=0.4545:gamma_g=0.4545:gamma_b=0.4545,vflip,crop=135:68:0:67,split[a][b];[b]vflip[b2];[a][b2]vstack,split[c][d];[d]hflip[d2];[d2][c]hstack[sp];[1:v]format=rgba[fl];[sp][fl]overlay=format=auto:repeatlast=0,scale=1080:1080:flags=lanczos,setsar=1,format=yuv420p[v]" \
  -map "[v]" -map 0:a \
  -c:v h264_videotoolbox -b:v 8000k -c:a aac -b:a 192k -ar 48000 \
  -movflags +faststart OUT.mp4
```

## Upload & replace flow (Thor, 18 Sep 2026)

- Uploading is done by the agent via the YouTube Data API, **directly
  public**.
- **Replacing, not duplicating**: one song = one public video. After the new
  render is up, delete ALL older versions of that song on the channel.
- No private/unlisted staging. Thor checks the links directly.
- Full write-up lives in issue #1 of this repo.

## Rules (learned the hard way)

- Re-rendering a published track: MATCH its look unless Thor asks for the
  new one. He rejects wrong-look re-renders ("VERY wrong", "Nope", "Try
  again").
- "It was going up before and it isnt now": the waterfall must scroll UP.
- Colours are ffmpeg presets; no colour forensics needed. When in doubt,
  show him a 2x2 sampler grid and let him pick.
- Scroll direction, mirror order (time first, then frequency) and flash
  dimensions must match the chain above exactly; the seam sizes are odd
  numbers and the flash is sized for the mirrored frame.
